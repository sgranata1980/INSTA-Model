# Visual language – Stefania

## Main direction (updated): high-fashion editorial
References: `editorial_bw_01.png` (main reference, Artlist asset uploaded), `runway_01.png` (mood only).
- **Black-and-white studio editorial** in the 90s style (Lindbergh school): grey seamless, large soft side light, silver-gelatin tonality, deep blacks, grain, unretouched skin, tousled hair
- Poses: sitting on the floor, leaning, relaxed instead of posed; direct, calm gaze
- Wardrobe: oversized black blazer as the only top, boots; tattoos are part of the image composition
- Runway moments as a second line (fashion shows, backstage), but always photographed realistically, never illustrative
- Street style (below) remains a complementary third line

## Core
- **Candid street-style editorial**, as if a street photographer caught her during Fashion Week, not posed
- **Light:** overcast, flat daylight, no direct sun, no flash
- **Palette:** muted, desaturated: warm stone grey, asphalt, black; accents only from nature (autumn leaves) or the amber indicators of the car
- **Lens:** telephoto (85–135 mm), compression, shot from across the street, eye level
- **Look:** real photograph, fine grain, natural skin and fabric texture, no HDR, no gloss, no oversaturation
- **Locations:** Frankfurt Westend/Nordend, Gründerzeit facades, red Main sandstone, wrought-iron gates, cobblestones, autumn leaves

## Wardrobe (signature look)
- Oversized black leather blazer, black turtleneck
- Black lace-pattern tights
- Black patent pointed slingbacks
- Small black suede shoulder bag with a gold clasp
- Small dark oval sunglasses
- Overall monochrome black, gold only in jewellery/watch

## Car
- Always pass `character/car/car_v1.png` as a reference; the car stands parked in the background, slightly out of focus, never as a showroom shot

## No-gos
- Night neon, HDR, glossy "AI" look
- Studio flash, posed model poses with hip pop
- Strong colours, filters
